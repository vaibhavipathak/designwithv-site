Design notes

  • One skill, practised for real: compare an AI draft with the call facts, then trust, edit or reject. It traces to action 3 on the action map.
  • Consequence first, then the reason: wrong choices show what would happen on the job, not just "incorrect".
  • Easy to hard: spot a wrong number first, then judge tone and promises.
  • Scoring that rewards judgment: a second try on the first decision earns no points, so 20/20 means you caught it the first time.
  • Accessible: real buttons, full keyboard use, focus moves to each feedback message, and feedback uses words and symbols, not colour alone.
